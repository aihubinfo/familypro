If your ChatGPT account is deactivated and a charge still appears, find **who billed you** before opening another support ticket. A subscription bought on chatgpt.com, through Apple's App Store, or through Google Play has a different cancellation route. An appeal asks OpenAI to review account access; cancellation stops future renewals. Neither action automatically refunds a past charge.

This guide reflects OpenAI's Help Center instructions checked on **October 10, 2026**. Save the deactivation notice, purchase receipt, and charge date. For the account decision itself, use our <a href="../chatgpt-account-deactivated-appeal-guide-2026/">ChatGPT deactivation appeal guide</a>.

## First, identify the billing platform

Search your email for OpenAI, ChatGPT, Apple, or Google Play receipts. Compare the receipt with the card statement and the subscription list for the account used to buy it. You may have more than one ChatGPT or app-store account.

| Where you bought it | Where to cancel |
| --- | --- |
| chatgpt.com | ChatGPT Billing settings, or OpenAI Support if you cannot get in |
| Apple App Store | Subscriptions under the Apple Account used for purchase |
| Google Play | Subscriptions under the Google Account used for purchase |

Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. OpenAI says this explicitly in its <a href="https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7232927-canceling-your-chatgpt-subscription" rel="nofollow">cancellation instructions</a>.

## Cancel through the platform that charged you

### Bought on chatgpt.com and can still sign in

In the purchasing account, go to **Settings → Billing → Cancel plan → Cancel**. Check the subscription status and next billing date afterward, and keep the confirmation. If the plan is missing, check that you used the original sign-in method and account.

### Bought on chatgpt.com and locked out

Open a request through the <a href="https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6614161-how-can-i-contact-support" rel="nofollow">OpenAI Help Center support chat</a>. Give the subscription email, the deactivation message, your purchase channel, and the recent charge date. OpenAI's cancellation guide asks for the email tied to the subscription **plus any two** of these details: the card's last four digits, card expiration date, billing address, or date of recent charges. Never send a full card number or password.

If you are also appealing the account decision, use the appeal link in OpenAI's notification email. Tell Support separately that you need to stop renewal. Check the actual billing status rather than assuming deactivation canceled a web subscription.

### Bought through Apple or Google Play

For Apple, open **Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ChatGPT → Cancel Subscription** on the Apple Account used to buy it. For Google Play, open **Play Store → Subscriptions → ChatGPT → Cancel subscription** under the purchasing Google Account. If ChatGPT is absent, check the account named on the receipt.

Store purchases are managed through their stores. In particular, deleting a ChatGPT account does not cancel an Apple subscription. If you lost access to the store account, use Apple's or Google's account recovery process.

## What about a charge that already happened?

Cancellation stops future renewals; it does not automatically refund earlier charges. For purchases on chatgpt.com or Google Play, follow OpenAI's <a href="https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7232895-how-do-i-request-a-refund-for-my-chatgpt-subscription" rel="nofollow">refund instructions</a>. Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Eligibility depends on the purchase and location; a deactivated account does not itself guarantee a refund.

If you did not authorize the charge, record its date and amount, contact official support, and discuss the transaction with your card issuer. Keep billing evidence out of public posts and third-party “recovery” sites.

## Two questions before you close the case

**Is deactivation the same as deletion?** No. OpenAI says a user-deleted account cannot be restored, and waiting 30 days does not reactivate a deactivated account. Deleting an account cancels subscriptions billed directly by ChatGPT, but not an Apple subscription. See the <a href="https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6378407-deleting-your-chatgpt-account" rel="nofollow">account deletion guidance</a>.

**When should I cancel?** OpenAI advises canceling at least 24 hours before the next billing date. Confirm that renewal is off in the billing platform that issued the receipt.
